Privacy Policy for JimothyPet

Effective date: July 27, 2026
Developer: Charoite Games (Robert Garnett)
App: JimothyPet (package com.jimothypet.app — Android / web virtual pet)

This Privacy Policy describes how JimothyPet (“the App”) handles information. JimothyPet is a local virtual-pet game. It is designed to keep your pet progress on your device and not to sell personal data.

1. Information we collect

JimothyPet does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or payment details inside the App.

The App may store the following information on your device only:

  • Game save data (pet stats, care progress, settings, unlocks)
  • Local preferences (for example sound mute, alert toggles, language, and sleep schedule)
  • Local notification scheduling data needed for optional care reminders

This information stays on your device unless you choose to back up, share, or transfer device data through your own tools (for example device backup).

2. Permissions

Depending on your device and settings, JimothyPet may request:

  • Notifications — to send optional care alerts (for example form changes, mess, boredom, or acting up). You can turn alerts off in the App or in system settings.
  • Schedule exact alarms / boot completed / wake lock (Android) — only as needed so scheduled care reminders can fire after you leave the App. The App does not use the restricted USE_EXACT_ALARM permission.

JimothyPet does not use location, contacts, camera, microphone, or SMS permissions for its core features.

3. How we use information

On-device data is used only to:

  • Run and restore your pet game
  • Remember your settings
  • Deliver optional local care notifications you enable

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. JimothyPet does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in its normal design. Local notifications are created on your device by the App / operating system; they are not used to profile you for ads.

If you download the App from Google Play, Google may process distribution, crash, and device information under Google’s own policies.

5. Children’s privacy

JimothyPet is a casual pet game and may be suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App keeps progress locally and does not require sign-in, it does not request child personal data.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game data remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the App, or reset progress in-app (where available). Uninstalling JimothyPet removes the App’s local data from that device (subject to any separate device backups you control).

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App functioning safely. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Please keep your device updated and protect access to your phone.

8. Your choices

  • Disable care alerts in the App or in Android notification settings
  • Clear app data or uninstall to remove local saves
  • Contact us with privacy questions using the email below

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
